# Pause scrubbing, a loading screen and longer buffers

Status: draft for review · Builds on `feat/rewind` (`2026-10-30-rewind.md`)
and PR #5 (`2026-10-30-buffering.md`) · Branch `feat/rewind-ui`, PR into
`feat/rewind` on the fork

Three changes to playback that the viewer sees. While paused, the viewer
can move along the programme bar without anything playing. A loading
screen covers the black picture while a channel starts or the stream
refills. The playback buffer can be set to 20 or 30 seconds.

## Decisions from the review

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Where does it go? | A new branch `feat/rewind-ui` from `feat/rewind`, with a PR into `feat/rewind`. After it is merged, it is added to `merge/rewind` too, so PR #1 carries it into `test/all-features`. |
| Pause | Scrub while paused; nothing plays until OK or Play. |
| Buffer lengths | Add 20 and 30 s. Auto grows up to 30 s. |
| Loading screen | A spinner with the relay's real buffer level. |
| Return to live while paused | Buttons under the programme bar on the pause screen. |
| The live panel on Back | Removed. Back does what it did before rewinding existed: with "Ask to quit with BACK" on, it asks whether to quit, so the panel could not be reached for many people. |

## Today, on `feat/rewind`

- **Pausing** opens the info bar, which hides after 6 s like any other
  time. Left and Right (and Rewind and Fast Forward) jump while paused,
  but each jump starts playing again at once.
- **The jump bar** and the info bar are both at the bottom of the screen.
  When both are shown, they draw over each other.
- **There is no loading indicator.** While a stream is requested, while
  the relay holds data back to fill its buffer, and while the server
  stalls, the screen is black or frozen with no hint.
- **The playback buffer** offers Auto, Off, 2, 5 and 10 s. Auto grows
  0 → 2 → 5 → 10 s after each stall. The relay holds at most 64 MB.

## Behaviour

### Paused: moving along the bar

While paused, the info bar stays on screen for the whole pause, with the
programme bar from `2026-10-30-rewind.md`. The filled part of the bar
ends at the **chosen moment**, which starts as the moment paused at.

| Key while paused | Rewind on | Rewind off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Left, Rewind | Chosen moment 10 s earlier, not before the oldest kept moment | Nothing |
| Right, Fast Forward | Chosen moment 30 s later, not past live | Same, back up to live |
| OK, Play/Pause | Plays from the chosen moment | Same |
| Back, chosen moment moved | Chosen moment back to where it was paused | Same |
| Back, chosen moment not moved | As before rewinding existed: the quit question or the channel list | Same |

- The chosen moment is a point in time, so it doesn't drift while the
  pause goes on. "Behind live" under the bar counts up while paused,
  for example "−4:10 behind live · 12:40 kept".
- Rewind off: what is kept starts at the pause, so the chosen moment can't
  go before the moment paused at.
- A plain pause with nothing kept (HLS, not MPEG-TS) shows the info bar
  without the gray part. Left and Right keep their usual jobs: channel
  list and guide.
- The title and times follow the chosen moment, so moving into the
  previous programme shows that programme.
- When the pause ends, the info bar hides after the usual 6 s.

### No live panel

The live panel of `2026-10-30-rewind.md` (Back while behind live) is
removed. The pause buttons replace it. While playing behind live, the way
back to live is Fast Forward, or OK to pause and then Return to live.

### Paused: the buttons

While paused, a row of buttons sits under the programme bar, with a hint
line under it:

```
  ❚❚ Paused                         −4:10 behind live
  20:00 Tagesschau                              20:15
  [████████▓▓▓▓▓▓|░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░░]
  −4:10 behind live · 12:40 kept           8 min left

  [▶ Play from here] [⏭ Return to live] [● Record]

  ←/→ move   ↓ buttons   OK play   BACK reset
```

| Button | Does | Shown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Play from here** | Plays from the chosen moment, like OK | Always while paused |
| **Return to live** | Plays live and shows "Live" | When something is kept |
| **Record** | Nothing yet, shown greyed out | When something is kept |

- **Focus:** the bar has the focus at first, so Left and Right keep moving
  the chosen moment.
  - Down moves the focus into the buttons, starting on Play from here.
  - In the buttons, Left and Right move between them and OK presses one.
  - Up or Back moves the focus back to the bar.
- **Hint line:** while the bar has the focus it reads
  "←/→ move · ↓ buttons · OK play · BACK reset". In the buttons it reads
  "↑ back to the bar". "BACK reset" only shows once the chosen moment
  was moved, and with nothing kept the parts about moving are left out.
- **Back:** in the buttons, back to the bar. With the chosen moment moved,
  back to the moment paused at. Otherwise as before rewinding existed:
  the quit question, or the channel list.
- **When playing:** the row and the hint hide with the pause.

### Playing: jumping

Rewind and Fast Forward work as on `feat/rewind`: presses within 0.6 s add
up, then playback continues from there. The jump bar opens only while the
info bar is hidden. A jump hides the info bar first, so the two bars never
overlap.

### Loading screen

When there is no moving picture for 0.5 s, a loading screen covers the
video. The 0.5 s keeps it from flickering on short gaps.

```
        ┌──────────────────────────┐
        │          ◌ ◌ ◌           │
        │   Buffering…  3 of 10 s  │
        │   [██████░░░░░░░░░░░░]   │
        └──────────────────────────┘
```

- **When:** a channel is playing, not paused, without an error, and
  either the stream address is still being requested, or the player
  reports loading, stalled or buffering.
- **Text:**
  - "Loading channel…" until the channel has played for the first time.
  - "Buffering…" after that.
  - While the relay is filling its buffer, it adds the level and a bar,
    for example "Buffering… 3 of 10 s". The level comes from the relay,
    not from Qt.
- **Picture:** the last frame stays, dimmed, behind a panel in the middle.
- **Other overlays:** the info bar and the channel list can open over it.
  The error screen replaces it.
- **End:** the loading screen hides as soon as the player reports a
  playing picture again.

### Playback buffer lengths

| Setting | Values | Stored as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Playback buffer** | *Auto* (default), *Off*, *2*, *5*, *10*, *20*, *30 seconds* | `playback/buffer` (−1, 0, 2, 5, 10, 20, 30), as today |

- Auto grows 0 → 2 → 5 → 10 → 20 → 30 s, one step after each stall.
- The relay's limit rises from 64 MB to 128 MB. 30 s of an 8 Mbit/s HD
  channel is about 30 MB, and of a 25 Mbit/s 4K channel about 94 MB.
- The row's description stays "N seconds behind live".

## Design

### Buffer level for the loading screen

- `StreamProxy::liveBuffer(relayUrl)` returns the live reader's buffer:
  `bufferedMs`, `targetMs` and `filling`. All three are zero when the
  session has no live reader.
- `TvhBackend` gains `Q_INVOKABLE QVariantMap bufferState()` for the
  playing channel. QML reads it every 0.5 s while the loading screen is
  shown.

### StreamBuffer

- `normalizeMode()` knows 20 and 30.
- The Auto steps become 0, 2, 5, 10, 20 and 30 s.
- `s_maxFull` becomes 128 MB.

### PlayerView

- `PlayerView` gains `loading` (the conditions above, including the
  0.5 s delay) and `startedOnce` (the channel has played once), and
  forwards `mediaStatus`.
- `scrubAtMs` is the chosen moment as a time, set on pause.
  - `scrubBy(deltaMs)` moves it within what is kept.
  - `resetScrub()` puts it back to the moment paused at.
  - `togglePause()` resumes from it: from disk when it is behind live,
    live when it is at live.

### WatchOverlay

- The info bar's timer doesn't run while paused.
- Left, Right, Rewind and Fast Forward call `scrubBy()` while paused, and
  `queueJump()` while playing.
- `queueJump()` hides the info bar.
- The programme bar's playing moment is `scrubAtMs` while paused.
- A new `LoadingScreen` sits in the overlay, under the info bar and over
  the video.
- The pause buttons and the hint are part of the info bar, shown while
  paused. Down, Up and Back move the focus between the bar and the
  buttons.

## Tests

| Test | Covers |
| --- | --- |
| `test_streambuffer` | `normalizeMode()` for 20 and 30; Auto growing to 30 s and no further; holding up to 128 MB |
| `test_streamproxy` | `liveBuffer()`: filling and its level while a buffer fills, zeros without a live reader |

The scrubbing, the loading screen and the bars need a check on the device:
- pause, Left and Right without playing, OK plays from the chosen moment,
  Back resets it;
- Back while behind live and unmoved asks to quit (or opens the channel
  list), as before;
- the pause buttons: Down into them, Play from here, Return to live, Up
  and Back back to the bar;
- the same with rewinding off: Left does nothing, Right up to live;
- the loading screen on a channel change, with "Loading channel…";
- the loading screen with "Buffering… N of M s" after a stall in Auto or
  a fixed buffer;
- no overlap between the jump bar and the info bar.

## Out of scope

- Showing the playback buffer level outside the loading screen.
- Watching while scrubbing (a moving preview).
